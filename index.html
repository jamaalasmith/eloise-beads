Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Eloise!

I started beading because I couldn't find a bracelet in the exact colors I wanted, so I made one. Then I made one for my friend. Then I kind of couldn't stop.

Bracelets are the quickest, but purses are my favorite even though one can take me a whole weekend. Right now my favorite combo is pink and teal with one yellow bead in the middle. No reason, it just looks right.

I check every piece for loose beads before it goes out.

How each piece gets made

STEP 1

Pick the beads

I dump out a big pile and sort colors until something clicks.

STEP 2

String it by hand

One bead at a time. Sometimes I start over if the pattern looks off.

STEP 3

Finish & send

Tie it off, double-check it, wrap it up, and send it to you.
